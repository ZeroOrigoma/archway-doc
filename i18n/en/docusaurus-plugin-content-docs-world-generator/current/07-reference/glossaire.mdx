# Glossary

> Definitions of World Generator's terms.

## Adjacency graph

The record of which provinces share a border. Rebuilt on demand and exported as `adjacencies.csv`. Painting provinces by hand invalidates it until you press **Apply new province**.

## Base noise cache

The raw noise field, stored separately from the final heightmap so painted influence can be reapplied over a regenerated world.

## Biome

An ecological classification — grassland, desert, deep ocean. Sixteen exist, each with a colour, a density modifier, a height offset and a set of strata it is allowed to occupy.

## Biome layer

An imported image that paints one biome. Layers form an ordered stack; those above paint over those below.

## Chaos

The irregularity of province boundaries. At 0 the result approaches a clean Voronoi tessellation; raising it makes borders look drawn rather than computed.

## Domain warping

Displacing noise coordinates before sampling. Turns rounded blobs into continents with inlets, peninsulas and fjords. Set in **Terrain → Shaping → Warped coastlines**.

## Falloff

The softness of a brush stroke's edge. 0 gives a hard edge. Also adjustable with `Alt` + scroll.

## FBm

Fractional Brownian motion — the default fractal mode. Sums octaves of noise at decreasing amplitude, giving rolling, natural terrain.

## Gain

How much each successive octave contributes. Lower gives smoother terrain.

## Hypsometric curve

A redistribution of elevation that makes plains dominate and high peaks rare, as in real terrain. Sea depth is left alone. Enabled under the name **Realistic elevation spread**.

## Influence map

Where hand sculpting is stored. Kept separate from the noise, which is why regenerating terrain does not erase what you painted.

## Lacunarity

How much finer each octave is than the last. 2.0 is the classical value — each octave has twice the frequency of the one before.

## Level

One tier of the territory hierarchy. Built on provinces or on another level. Each level exports to its own CSV, named after it.

## Mask

An image that drives part of the world instead of the noise. Two kinds: **stratum overrides**, which replace an elevation band's shape, and **biome layers**, which paint biomes.

## Micro-province

A province below a size threshold, usually a sliver along a coastline. Removed by **Provinces → Cleanup**.

## Octave

One layer of noise. More octaves means more detail and more generation time.

## Projection

How the 2D map relates to a sphere. Planar (none), Equirectangular (latitude/longitude), or Mercator (conformal cylindrical).

## Province

The smallest unit of the map. Generated in two independent populations, land and sea. Everything in the hierarchy is built on them.

## Ridged

A fractal mode that inverts and sharpens each octave, producing ridgelines and mountain chains rather than hills.

## Seed

The number that determines a world. The same seed with the same settings always produces the same map, on any machine.

## Stencil

An imported image used as a reference to trace, or as a source for flood-filling terrain. Distinct from a mask: a stencil is what you work *from*, a mask is what the generator works *with*.

## Stratum

One of the nine elevation bands, from *Abyssal* to *High peaks*. Sea level sits between *Coastal waters* and *Low plains*.

## Stratum slope

An export setting controlling how sharp the boundary between elevation bands is in the heightmap. 0 keeps hard terraces; raising it bevels them.

## Territory

A group of provinces, or a group of smaller territories, within one hierarchy level.

## `.wgen`

The document format. A single binary file holding a world's complete state — settings, terrain, painted influence, provinces, hierarchy, masks and stencil. Currently version 24.
