# The interface

The window is three regions: a **toolbar** across the top, a **panel** on the left, the **map** filling the rest.

## The title bar

Drawn by Windows, but tinted to the application's colours: the window reads as one surface, not as a dark tool under a light strip. On Windows 10 it falls back to the system dark caption.

## The toolbar

<Capture
  src="/img/wgen/01-barre-outils.png"
  caption="File, then history"
  fig="Fig. 9.2"
/>

| Control | Shortcut | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **New world** | — | Asks for confirmation if the current world has unsaved changes |
| **Open…** | — | Opens a sheet listing the contents of `saves/` |
| **Save** | `Ctrl`+`S` | Saves in place; asks for a name the first time |
| **Save as…** | — | Always asks for a name |
| **Undo** | `Ctrl`+`Z` (or `Ctrl`+`W`) | One history, map and hierarchy alike |
| **Redo** | `Ctrl`+`Y` | |

The centre shows the **document name** and the **resolution**. The right shows a spinner and *Generating* while a background job runs.

:::info A greyed-out button is not broken
New, Open, Undo and Redo take an exclusive lock on the world. During a generation or a preview rebuild they are disabled rather than left to freeze the window. The capsule in the bottom-right corner says what is being recomputed.
:::

## The panel

### The tabs

<Capture
  src="/img/wgen/02-onglets.png"
  caption="Seven panels, in pipeline order"
  fig="Fig. 9.3"
/>

| Panel | Role |
|---|---|
| **Terrain** | Noise, projection and world size |
| **Sculpt** | Paint elevation and biomes by hand |
| **Provinces** | Subdivide the map |
| **Hierarchy** | Group provinces into territories |
| **Masks** | Import and export mask layers |
| **Export** | Write maps and tables to disk |
| **Settings** | Interface size and shortcuts |

Switching tabs has side effects, deliberately: entering **Sculpt** switches the view to topography and selects the brush; entering **Hierarchy** selects the highest level and shows it.

### The view switcher

Just below the panel title, and pinned — it does not scroll.

<Capture
  src="/img/wgen/03-selecteur-vue.png"
  caption="The view switcher, and the options belonging to the current view"
  fig="Fig. 9.4"
/>

| View | Shows | Available |
|---|---|---|
| **Biome** | Ecological colouring | Always |
| **Topo** | The nine elevation bands | Always |
| **Provinces** | One distinct colour per province | Once provinces exist |
| **Territories** | The hierarchy levels | Once a level exists |

Views with no data behind them are **hidden rather than shown disabled**.

Switching view is not decorative: the brush paints into whatever the current view represents. In **Biome** it writes biome identifiers; in **Topo**, strata.

#### Pinned overlays

**`Ctrl`+click** a view button to pin that view as a translucent layer on top of the current one. An amber dot appears on the pinned button.

That is how you check one layer against another — province boundaries over topography, territory borders over biomes.

Two controls then appear: **Overlay opacity** and **Remove overlay**. `Ctrl`+clicking again removes the layer.

### Collapsible groups

Panel content is organised into groups you can fold away. The one or two groups a panel exists for open straight away; the rest start closed.

- **Click the header**, anywhere along the strip, to open or close it.
- Groups remember their state for the session.
- Headers are keyboard-reachable: `Tab` to one, `Space` or `Enter` to toggle it.

### The action bar

### Resizing

Drag the divider between the panel and the map. The cursor becomes a horizontal double arrow over it.

## The map

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **Scroll** | Zoom, centred on the pointer. Range 10 % – 5000 % |
| **Middle-drag** | Pan |
| **Left-click** | Paint, or select — depends on the active tool |
| **Right-click** | Pick, or stop painting |

While the **Sculpt** tab is open, a reticle follows the pointer showing the brush's size, shape and falloff.

## Modal sheets

Save, Open and the two file browsers appear as sheets over a dimmed window. While a sheet is open, everything behind it still draws but ignores the pointer.

`Escape` closes a sheet. It does **not** quit the application.

## Notifications

| Element | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Toast** | Bottom of the map | Transient confirmation — *Seed 4125 copied* |
| **Capsule** | Bottom right | Names what is being recomputed in the background |
| **Progress card** | Centre | Long generations, with a percentage |
| **Tooltip** | Under the pointer | After a short dwell on an icon button |

## The nine strata

| # | Stratum | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Abyssal** | Near-black blue |
| 1 | **Oceanic** | Deep blue |
| 2 | **Lagoon** | Mid blue |
| 3 | **Coastal waters** | Bright blue — the last water band |
| 4 | **Low plains** | Green |
| 5 | **Hills** | Yellow-green |
| 6 | **Plateaus** | Light brown |
| 7 | **Mountainous** | Dark brown |
| 8 | **High peaks** | White |

Sea level sits between **Coastal waters** and **Low plains**.

## The sixteen biomes

| Land | Water |
|---|---|
| Grassland, Forest, Jungle | Coastal Waters |
| Rocky Wastes, Savanna, Desert | High Seas |
| Snow, Swamp, Tundra, Alpine | Deep Ocean, River, Lake |

Biomes are constrained by elevation: **Snow** and **Rocky Wastes** to the three highest bands, **Swamp** to the low and coastal ones, **Deep Ocean** to the abyssal band alone. Those constraints are edited in **Sculpt → Allowed strata**.

## Interface size

| Shortcut | Effect |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl` `+` | Increase by 25 % |
| `Ctrl` `-` | Decrease by 25 % |
| `Ctrl` `0` | Match the display's own scaling |

The shortcuts clamp at 75 % and 300 %. **Settings → Interface size** offers presets across the useful middle of that range, 100 % to 200 %, plus a **Match the display** button. The choice persists in `ui_scale.cfg`.
