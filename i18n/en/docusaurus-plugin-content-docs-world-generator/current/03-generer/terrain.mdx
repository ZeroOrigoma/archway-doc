# The Terrain panel

The first panel, and the one everything else is built on. It turns a seed and a handful of noise settings into a heightmap, and that heightmap into elevation strata and biomes.

**Primary action** — *Generate map*, in the bar pinned at the bottom of the panel. The dice button beside it rolls a new seed first.

## Seed

*Open by default.*

The seed identifies a world. The same seed with the same settings always produces the same map, on any machine.

| Control | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Seed** | Typed directly. Range 0 – 999,999,999 |
| **Copy** | Puts the seed on the clipboard |
| **Paste** | Reads a seed from the clipboard, tolerating decoration — `Seed: 4125`, `world 4125 (v2)` and `4125` all work |

### Saved seeds

A shelf for worlds worth coming back to. Type a name, **Keep**, and the seed is stored in `seeds.txt` next to the application.

- Click a row to reload that seed.
- The **✕** on a row removes it.
- **Keep** is disabled when the current seed is already on the shelf.

`seeds.txt` is plain text, editable and shareable. Malformed lines are skipped one by one rather than causing the file to be discarded.

## Resolution

*Collapsed by default.*

Resolution is **independent of window size**. Resizing the window changes what you see, not what you generate.

| Control | Range |
|---|---|
| **Width** | 100 – 16384 |
| **Height** | 100 – 16384 |

### Presets

| Preset | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **1080p** | 1920 × 1080 | Iterating on settings — a few seconds per generation |
| **4K** | 3840 × 2160 | A detailed map that is still quick to regenerate |
| **Archway** | 8129 × 4065 | Production size for an Archway project |

The note below reports what the chosen size costs in memory, at roughly 25 bytes per pixel. Above **2 GB** the application asks for confirmation.

## Noise

*Collapsed by default.*

| Control | Range | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Scale** | 1 – 20 | Feature size. Low = few large masses; high = archipelago |
| **Octaves** | 1 – 10 | Layers of detail. 3 is smooth, 8 is craggy. Costs generation time |
| **Sea level** | 0 – 1 | Where water ends and land begins |
| **Edge mask** | 0 – 1 | Fades elevation toward the borders |
| **Lacunarity** | 1 – 5 | How much finer each octave is than the last. 2.0 is the classical value |
| **Gain** | 0 – 1 | How much each octave contributes. Lower is smoother |

### Noise type

| Type | Character |
|---|---|
| **Simplex** | Smooth, organic, no directional artefacts |
| **Cellular** | Worley/Voronoi — cracked, tessellated, good for shattered terrain |
| **Perlin** | The classic. Slight axis alignment, familiar look. **Default** |

### Fractal type

| Type | Character |
|---|---|
| **FBm** | Fractional Brownian motion — rolling, natural. **Default** |
| **Ridged** | Inverted and sharpened: ridgelines and mountain chains |
| **Ping pong** | Folded: terraced and banded terrain |

## Projection

*Collapsed by default.*

| Projection | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Planar** | A flat map. No spherical distortion |
| **Equirect.** | Latitude/longitude grid — the standard world-map layout |
| **Mercator** | Conformal cylindrical, stretched toward the poles |

### Seamless longitude

Appears for the two spherical projections.

**On**: the map wraps east to west, as a globe does, and the edge mask only fades the poles.

**Off**: the mask fades all four sides, which cuts a strip of ocean down the antimeridian and breaks the wrap. The panel warns you when that is the case.

## Shaping

*Collapsed by default.*

Each option here changes the shape of a world, so none is on by default: a seed keeps producing what it always produced until you ask for something else.

### Warped coastlines

Displaces the noise before sampling it. Fractal noise alone makes rounded blobs; warping it gives continents with inlets, peninsulas and fjords.

| Control | Range |
|---|---|
| **Warp strength** | 0 – 1 |
| **Warp scale** | 0.1 – 2 |

### Realistic elevation spread

Applies a hypsometric curve — plains dominate, high peaks become rare, the way real elevation is distributed. Sea depth is left alone.

| Control | Range | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Lowland bias** | 1 – 3 | Higher flattens more aggressively |

:::warning This moves your coastline
The curve is applied **before** the edge mask, so it can move samples across sea level. Switching it on changes your shoreline, not just your mountains.
:::

## Recipes

### A continent, not an archipelago
Scale 3–5, Edge mask 0.6 or more, Warped coastlines on with strength around 0.4.

### A mountain range
**Ridged** fractal, Octaves 6–8, Gain 0.5.

### A shattered, non-terrestrial world
**Cellular** noise, **FBm** fractal, Lacunarity 2.5.

### An Earth-like globe
**Equirect.** projection, Seamless longitude **on**, Realistic elevation spread **on** with a lowland bias around 2.
